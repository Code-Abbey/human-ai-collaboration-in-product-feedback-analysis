## Version details

The InsightBench preprint first appeared in 2024. The cited Version 4 is dated 27 February 2025, so this bibliography uses 2025 and identifies [that version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06423v4).

The Yan et al. citation uses the [2023 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03999v1), as in the concept. A [conference version](https://doi.org/10.1145/3613905.3650732) appeared in 2024. These are versions of the same work, rather than separate studies.
