# References

These sources describe the datasets and relevant approaches to app-review analysis. The full citation details are saved in [references.bib](references.bib). Bibliographic details were checked on 18 October 2026.

## Dataset sources and related papers

**GenAI-EdSent / Kahoot!**

- [Dataset on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Erfan-Nourbakhsh/GenAI-EdSent), by Erfan Nourbakhsh.
- Mazaherian and Nourbakhsh (2025), *Unveiling User Perceptions in the Generative AI Era: A Sentiment-Driven Evaluation of AI Educational Apps' Role in Digital Transformation of e-Teaching*. [DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2512.11934](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2512.11934).

The paper explains the educational-app review project. It is an arXiv preprint, meaning it is available as a research manuscript. Publication in a peer-reviewed venue has not been verified. Its DOI identifies the paper, not a separate dataset record.

**Top 15 / Spotify and Zoom**

- Asnawi et al. (2023), *User Feedback Dataset from the Top 15 Downloaded Mobile Applications*. [Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.10204232](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10204232).
- Asnawi et al. (2023), *The Combination of Contextualized Topic Model and MPNet for User Feedback Topic Modeling*, IEEE Access. [Paper DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3332644](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3332644).

The dataset provides the reviews. The related paper presents a method for grouping feedback into topics.

**RARE**

- Singh (2024), *RARE*, version 2. [Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.13939427](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13939427).
- Singh et al. (2024), *Refining App Reviews: Dataset, Methodology, and Evaluation*, EMNLP Industry Track. [Paper DOI: 10.18653/v1/2024.emnlp-industry.44](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.emnlp-industry.44).

The dataset and paper focus on rewriting individual reviews. They provide background for this thesis, whose main task is comparing reports based on groups of reviews.

## Background on review analysis

- Maalej and Nabil (2015), *Bug report, feature request, or simply praise? On automatically classifying app reviews*. [DOI: 10.1109/RE.2015.7320414](https://doi.org/10.1109/RE.2015.7320414). Useful for choosing review categories.
- Guzman and Maalej (2014), *How Do Users Like This Feature? A Fine Grained Sentiment Analysis of App Reviews*. [DOI: 10.1109/RE.2014.6912257](https://doi.org/10.1109/RE.2014.6912257). Useful for understanding opinions about particular app features.
