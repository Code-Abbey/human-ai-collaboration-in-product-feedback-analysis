# Research questions and objectives

The following research questions and objectives were submitted to my supervisor.

## Main research question

How does a Human-in-the-Loop AI workflow compare with manual analysis and AI-only analysis in producing clear, useful, and actionable product feedback insights from app reviews?

## Sub-questions

1. How do manually created feedback reports, AI-only reports, and Human-in-the-Loop refined reports differ in terms of clarity, usefulness, and actionability?
2. What types of human refinements are most commonly needed when working with AI-generated feedback themes, summaries, and suggested product actions?
3. How do expert evaluators assess the quality of feedback reports created through manual analysis, AI-only analysis, and AI-assisted human refinement?

## Objectives

1. Design and implement a small AI/NLP pipeline for analysing app reviews, building on prior work in app review mining (Dąbrowski et al., 2022).
2. Generate AI-based categories, themes, summaries, and product improvement suggestions from a selected review dataset.
3. Design a refinement process that allows users to correct, edit, merge, and split AI-generated outputs, informed by Human-AI collaboration in thematic analysis (Yan et al., 2023).
4. Compare manual analysis, AI-only analysis, and AI-assisted human refinement.
5. Evaluate the final reports using clarity, usefulness, actionability, relevance to the original reviews, and perceived workload.

## How the study will answer the questions

| Sub-question | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|
| 1. Differences between reports | Expert scores for clarity, usefulness and actionability across the three approaches. |
| 2. Common human refinements | A record of corrections, edits, merges and splits, with counts and examples for categories, themes, summaries and suggested actions. |
| 3. Expert assessment | Experts' report scores and short written reasons, checked against the original reviews. |

Relevance to the original reviews will also be rated. Participants producing manual and human-refined reports will report their perceived workload after each task. Time spent will be recorded separately.
