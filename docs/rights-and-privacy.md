# Data permissions and privacy

The datasets come from published research sources. The planned use is non-commercial thesis research.

| Dataset | Source licence |
|---|---|
| GenAI-EdSent / Kahoot! | CC BY-NC 4.0 |
| Top 15 / Spotify and Zoom | CC BY 4.0 in the metadata; the description also mentions non-commercial use |
| RARE | CC BY 4.0 |

The source authors and related papers are credited in the [references](../references/README.md). The licences require attribution; CC BY-NC also limits commercial use. Source licences do not remove privacy obligations.

Raw reviews are kept locally and are not uploaded to the public repository. The repository provides download links, scripts and summary findings.

The selected study data will exclude usernames, profile images and other unnecessary personal fields. Review text will also be checked for names, contact details and sensitive information. Automatic checks alone cannot remove every personal detail.

Storage, access and deletion arrangements will be agreed before the participant study. Data sent to an external AI service will first need an appropriate privacy check.
