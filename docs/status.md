# Progress and next steps

Updated 18 October 2026.

## Current stage

This thesis is at the planning and dataset-selection stage. The researcher has not yet downloaded the thesis dataset. The final dataset and number of reviews have not been decided.

The review count in the concept was an initial proposal, not a fixed target or upper limit. The study may use more reviews. The final number will depend on the selected dataset, the analysis requirements, the time available and discussion with the supervisor.

## Prepared so far

- The concept, research questions and objectives have been documented.
- Possible datasets, source links and relevant references have been identified.
- A proposed workflow, evaluation approach and project plan have been outlined.

The existing feasibility reports are preliminary supporting material from earlier automated source checks. They are not a record of the researcher's dataset download, final data preparation or thesis analysis.

## Next steps

1. Confirm which dataset to use and download it for the thesis.
2. Inspect its contents and agree a suitable number of reviews.
3. Clean the selected reviews and prepare the study data.
4. Build the AI pipeline and dashboard.
5. Run the manual, AI-only and Human-in-the-Loop comparison.
6. Evaluate the reports and, where feasible, record perceived workload, time spent and human refinements.

The prototype, main analysis and participant evaluation have not yet been completed. The [project plan](project-plan.md) describes the proposed stages.
